## Summary of Main Results

Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a simple Lie algebra over an algebraically closed field $k$ of characteristic $0$, with adjoint group $G$, Cartan subalgebra $\mathfrak{h}$ of rank $\operatorname{rk}\mathfrak{g}$, Weyl group $W$, dual Coxeter number $h^\vee$, and Killing form $B$. Let $\mathcal{C}^d_{\mathfrak{g}} \subset \mathfrak{g}^d$ be the $d$-fold commuting scheme defined by the ideal $I_d$ generated by the coordinates of $[x_i, x_j]$ ($1 \le i < j \le d$), and let

$$\Phi : k[\mathcal{C}^d_{\mathfrak{g}}]^G \longrightarrow k[\mathfrak{h}^d]^W$$

be the higher-dimensional Chevalley restriction map. Form the $d \times d$ Killing Gram determinant $\det T := \det_{1 \le i,j \le d} B(x_i, x_j) \in k[\mathfrak{g}^d]^{G \times \mathrm{SL}_d}$ of degree $2d$.

### 1. Exact Multiplicity of the $(2^d)$ Schur Sector via Odd-to-Even CDSW Transfer (Theorem 2.3)

For any finite-dimensional super vector space $E$, polynomial functoriality gives $\mathcal{O}(\mathcal{C}_E)^G_{(2^d)} \cong \mathbb{S}_{(2^d)}(E) \otimes L_d$ for a vector space $L_d$ independent of $E$. Substituting $x_i = X u_i + Y v_i$ transfers the Cachazo–Douglas–Seiberg–Witten / Kumar theorem ($S^{h^\vee - 1} \ne 0$, $S^{h^\vee} = 0$ for $S = B(X,Y)$ on $\mathcal{O}(\mathcal{C}_{\Pi k^2})^G$) to even commuting schemes via the Grassmann Gram identity $\det_{1 \le i,j \le d}(u_i v_j + u_j v_i) = (d+1)!\prod_{i=1}^d u_i v_i$ (Lemma 3.1):

- For $1 \le d < h^\vee$, $\dim L_d = 1$ and the $\mathrm{SL}_d$-invariant degree-$2d$ subspace of $k[\mathcal{C}^d_{\mathfrak{g}}]^G$ is spanned by $\det T \notin I_d$.
- For $d \ge h^\vee$, $\dim L_d = 0$ and $\det T \in I_d$.

### 2. Counterexamples to the Chen–Ngô Conjectures (Corollary 2.4)

Because $\operatorname{rk}(B|_{\mathfrak{h}}) = \operatorname{rk}\mathfrak{g}$, the restriction $\Phi(\det T) = \det_{1 \le i,j \le d} B|_{\mathfrak{h}}(x_i, x_j)$ vanishes identically whenever $d > \operatorname{rk}\mathfrak{g}$. Consequently, for every pair $(\mathfrak{g}, d)$ satisfying

$$\operatorname{rk}\mathfrak{g} < d < h^\vee,$$

the image of $\det T$ in $k[\mathcal{C}^d_{\mathfrak{g}}]^G$ is a **nonzero nilpotent $G$-invariant with zero Cartan restriction** ($\det T \in \ker\Phi \setminus \{0\}$). This disproves Conjectures 3.1 and 3.2 of Chen–Ngô (*Duke Math. J.* **169** (2020), 1971–2004, [`arXiv:1905.04741`](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.04741)) across every simple Lie type with $\operatorname{rk}\mathfrak{g} + 1 < h^\vee$:

| Type | $\operatorname{rk}\mathfrak{g}$ | $h^\vee$ | Counterexample window $\operatorname{rk}\mathfrak{g} < d < h^\vee$ |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| $A_n$ ($n \ge 1$), $C_n$ ($n \ge 1$) | $n$ | $n+1$ | empty |
| $B_2$ | $2$ | $3$ | empty |
| $B_n$ ($n \ge 3$, $\mathfrak{so}_{2n+1}$) | $n$ | $2n-1$ | $n+1 \le d \le 2n-2$ (smallest: $\mathfrak{so}_7$, $d=4$, degree $8$) |
| $D_n$ ($n \ge 4$, $\mathfrak{so}_{2n}$) | $n$ | $2n-2$ | $n+1 \le d \le 2n-3$ (smallest: $\mathfrak{so}_8$, $d=5$, degree $10$) |
| $G_2$ | $2$ | $4$ | $d = 3$ (degree $6$) |
| $F_4$ | $4$ | $9$ | $5 \le d \le 8$ |
| $E_6$ | $6$ | $12$ | $7 \le d \le 11$ |
| $E_7$ | $7$ | $18$ | $8 \le d \le 17$ |
| $E_8$ | $8$ | $30$ | $9 \le d \le 29$ |

### 3. Direct Disproof of Song–Xia–Xu (*Adv. Math.* 2023, `arXiv:2207.03147`) (Proposition 6.1 & Corollary 6.4)

Since $\det T = \det_{i,j} \operatorname{tr}(x_i x_j)$ (up to nonzero scalar) is invariant under the full orthogonal group $O_n$, the table above for $B_m$ ($m \ge 3$) and $D_m$ ($m \ge 4$) directly disproves **Theorem 1.2(1)** and **Corollary 1.3** of L. Song, X. Xia, and J. Xu, *A higher-dimensional Chevalley restriction theorem for orthogonal groups*, *Adv. Math.* **426** (2023), 109104 ([`arXiv:2207.03147`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.03147)) for both $G = O_n$ and $G = SO_n$ for every $n \ge 7$ (already at $n = 7, d = 4$).

Section 6 further disproves their key technical input (**Lemma 5.2** of `arXiv:2207.03147`) directly for **every $d \ge 2$**:

- **Proposition 6.1:** On the Pfaffian hypersurface $h_{2d+2} = 0$ of the generic $(2d+3)\times(2d+3)$ skew-symmetric matrix $(t_{ij})$, the $2d \times 2d$ matrix $A^{\mathrm{SXX}}(i,j) = h_{i,j,2d+2}^2 + h_{i,2d+1,2d+2}^2 + h_{j,2d+1,2d+2}^2$ factors through a $4 \times 4$ symmetric matrix $M$ with $\det M = 0$, hence satisfies $\operatorname{rk} A^{\mathrm{SXX}} \le 3 < 2d$ everywhere on $h_{2d+2} = 0$. By irreducibility of the Pfaffian, $\det A^{\mathrm{SXX}} \in (h_{2d+2})$ for every $d \ge 2$ (and in every characteristic $\ne 2$).
- **Remark 6.3 (Exact location of the gap):** In the induction on `arXiv:2207.03147v3`, p. 17, $\det A^{\mathrm{SXX}}$ has $t_{12}$-degree $4d - 2$ (e.g. $6$ at $d=2$ and $10$ at $d=3$) rather than $\le 4d - 4$, because for $j \ge 3$ the entries $A^{\mathrm{SXX}}(1,j)$ and $A^{\mathrm{SXX}}(2,j)$ contain $h_{j,2d+1,2d+2}^2$, which is quadratic in $t_{12}$; moreover, the coefficient of $t_{12}^{4d-4}$ does not factor as claimed.

### 4. Super-Commuting Schemes and 4d $\mathcal{N}=4$ Super-Yang–Mills BPS Cohomology (Section 7)

Applying Theorem 2.3 to $E = k^{3|2}$ produces an irreducible $\mathfrak{gl}(3|2)$-multiplet $\mathbb{S}_{(2^d)}(k^{3|2}) \otimes L_d \subset \mathcal{O}(\mathcal{C}^{3|2}_{\mathfrak{g}})^G$ of non-Cartan quarter-BPS cohomology classes whenever $\operatorname{rk}\mathfrak{g} < d < h^\vee$ (Corollary 7.1), unifying the purely scalar $G_2$ class at level $12$, the $\mathfrak{so}_7$ non-Cartan class responsible for the classical $(\mathfrak{so}_7, \mathfrak{sp}_6)$ S-duality mismatch at level $18$, and the non-Coulomb gaugino power $[S^d]$ in a single $\mathfrak{gl}(3|2)$-module.
